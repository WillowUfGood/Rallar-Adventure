The Rallarvegen (the Navvies' Road) is designated as a cultural monument and important part of Norwegian National Heritage. It has been named Norway's most beautiful bike ride several times. This is a journey through powerful nature along beautiful lakes and between high mountains. It lies at the foot of two national parks, Hallingskarvet and Hardangervidda National Parks. The way the road is built and maintained is still in keeping with what was typical of that time, and today everyone can admire the impressive work done on walls and bridges in the early 20th century. The road runs parallel with the Bergen railway and is closed for vehicles, it continues purely as a trail/cycle-track.

Our base-camp and headquarters is at Vatnahalsen hotel. We have been working, on and along the Rallarvegen, since 2020, and we would love to show you just how great it is up here away from the stress of town life.

Our mission at RallarAdventure is to share the experience of the Norwegian mountains with our guests. We are passionate about nature and sustainability, that is why we love working in this area where there are no cars, no roads, no pollution! Only accessible by train. We promote climate friendly travel.

Our ecological responsibility: We take pride in promoting sustainable tourism. We use no carbon emitting vehicles and getting to us is only possible by train, which is electric driven and the electricity comes from clean hydroelectricity from the valley. At RallarAdventure we respect nature reserves, always leave campsites clean and tidy and always dispose of your rubbish properly, recycling when possible. When no better solution is available, climate impact is 100% carbon offset by planting trees with Ecotree, Anfibio and Sitew.
